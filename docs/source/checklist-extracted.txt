Part B. SPI-RDT Checklist
Part B constitutes the main body of the SPI-RDT Checklist for conducting the assessment and scoring. The Checklist is laid out in five sections that align with the national standard requirements.
Instructions as per guidelines
For each question, answer as follows:
Tick Yes (Y), only if full compliance is demonstrated
Tick Partial (P), if some compliance is demonstrated
Tick No (N), if there is no evidence of compliance
Tick Not Applicable (N/A), if the question is not applicable or irrelevant to the country context, a score is not required
In the Comment box, describe the specific gap (Partial and No scores) or the reason the question does not apply (N/A)
Sections 1 (Organization and Management), 2 (Physical Facility and Equipment) and 3 (Safety) pertain to the overall facility incorporating the testing sites. Section 4 (Testing) assesses the testing site(s) and Section 5 (Specimen Referral) assesses the testing site(s).

PART C: Scoring
Each element marked will be assigned a point value: ￼
Items marked “Yes or Y” receive 2 points each
Items marked “Partial or P” receive 1 point each
Items marked “No or N” receive 0 point each
Total points scored for each section should be tallied and recorded at the end of each section. The possible total number of points obtained for all 5 sections will depend on the numbers of test types and where N/A is observed. Refer to guidelines for detailed instructions.
